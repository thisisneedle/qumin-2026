Why Weibo Still Matters for Western Brands

Sina Weibo is China's open social network, the place where trends ignite, celebrities command millions of followers and consumers voice opinions that move markets. For Western brands, it offers something rare: a public stage where brand narrative and consumer conversation happen side by side.

A strong Weibo marketing strategy is not about posting frequently. It is about owning a credible point of view, working with the right voices at the right moments and connecting platform activity to broader China brand goals. Advertising on Weibo amplifies what already resonates; without that foundation, spend is wasted.

Qumin has built and executed Weibo strategies for luxury, lifestyle and consumer brands entering or scaling in China. We bring platform knowledge, cultural fluency and brand discipline together so your presence on Weibo earns attention rather than just buying it.
