Common questions

What does a China marketing agency actually do for a Western brand?

A specialist China marketing agency like Qumin covers everything from initial market-entry strategy and brand positioning through to ongoing social media management on platforms like Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat and Weibo. We also handle content creation, KOL and influencer programmes and consumer research.

How is Qumin different from other Chinese advertising agencies or local China agencies?

Most China agencies are built to serve Chinese brands or to handle media buying and performance channels. Qumin was founded specifically to work with Western consumer brands, so we are fluent in the internal language of a brand team in London, New York or Paris as well as the platform culture of Douyin or Xiaohongshu. We focus on brand marketing: strategy, social, content and KOL.

Which platforms does your China digital marketing agency work on?

Our core platforms are Xiaohongshu (RED), Douyin, WeChat and Weibo. These cover the primary touchpoints for discovery, community, content and loyalty in China for most consumer categories. Both. We have launched brands in China with no existing presence, as we did with Vuori, building the full market-entry strategy and initial social footprint from the ground up. We have also worked with established brands like Stone Island over 15-plus years to evolve their China social approach as the market changes.

What consumer brand categories does your China advertising agency specialise in?

Our strongest experience is in fashion, beauty and skincare, lifestyle, watches and jewellery, leisure and consumer technology.
