Common questions

What does a Xiaohongshu marketing agency actually do for a Western brand?

A specialist Xiaohongshu agency handles everything from platform strategy and account positioning to content creation, KOL and KOC sourcing, campaign management and performance analysis. For Western brands the most important first step is making sure your brand narrative is adapted for a Chinese audience before any influencer activity begins.

What is the difference between a KOL and a KOC, and which do I need?

KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders) are high-reach influencers, often celebrities or category experts, who drive awareness quickly. KOCs (Key Opinion Consumers) are everyday users whose authentic reviews carry strong trust signals in the Xiaohongshu algorithm and among peer audiences.

Can you work as our KOL agency in China if we already have a global agency?

Yes. Many of our clients have a global or regional agency handling other markets. We operate as their dedicated China KOL and social partner, plugging into global brand guidelines while applying the China-specific platform knowledge, influencer relationships and cultural nuance that a non-specialist agency typically cannot provide.

Is Xiaohongshu (RED / RedNote / Little Red Book) right for my brand category?

Xiaohongshu started in beauty and fashion but now covers lifestyle, wellness, travel, food, consumer tech, watches and jewellery and more. If your audience includes Chinese women aged 18 to 40 or younger affluent male consumers, RED is almost certainly part of the picture.

How do I know if a KOL agency in China is the right fit for a Western consumer brand?

Look for an agency that understands both the Chinese platform landscape and Western brand values, because the failure mode for most Western brands is either a local agency that ignores brand integrity or a Western agency that lacks genuine China platform expertise.
