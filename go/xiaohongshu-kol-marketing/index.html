Why KOL Marketing in China Needs a Different Playbook

Influencer marketing in China is not a translation of what works on Instagram. Xiaohongshu rewards genuine discovery content over polished advertising. Douyin rewards native storytelling at pace. Chinese KOLs operate inside platform ecosystems, community norms and trust dynamics that have no direct Western equivalent. Dropping a global influencer brief into this environment rarely works.
