Why Your China Market Entry Strategy Needs a Specialist

Western consumer brands that succeed in China share one thing: they treat it as a distinct market, not an extension of their global playbook. Chinese consumers are platform-native, culturally specific and deeply sceptical of brands that feel imported rather than invited. A China market entry strategy that works has to be built on genuine consumer research, sharp local positioning and a channel plan tuned to how Chinese audiences actually discover and trust brands.
