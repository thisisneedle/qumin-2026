Common questions

What does a WeChat marketing agency actually do for a Western brand?

A specialist WeChat marketing agency like Qumin handles the strategy, content, community and campaign activity that makes your Official Account a genuine brand asset. That includes defining your content pillars, writing and designing posts in Chinese, planning and running KOL partnerships, managing Moments and in-feed advertising, and reporting on what is working.

Do I need a WeChat Official Account before you can help?

Not necessarily. Many of our clients come to us before they have set up their WeChat presence at all, and we guide them through account registration, content strategy and launch planning from the beginning. If you already have an account that is underperforming, we audit what exists, identify the gaps and rebuild the approach. Either way, we start with strategy before touching execution.

How is WeChat advertising different from advertising on other platforms?

WeChat advertising sits inside a closed, trust-based ecosystem. Users are highly resistant to content that feels out of place or purely promotional. Effective WeChat advertising, whether Moments ads or Official Account placements, needs to feel native to the platform and connected to a credible brand presence.

Can you manage WeChat alongside our other China social channels?

Yes, and in most cases we recommend it. Chinese consumers move fluidly between WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Douyin and Weibo, and your brand story needs to be coherent across all of them while being adapted for each platform's distinct culture and format.

What kind of Western brands do you work with as a WeChat agency?

Our clients are predominantly Western consumer brands in fashion, beauty, lifestyle, watches and jewellery, leisure and consumer tech. We have worked with brands including Stone Island, Panerai, Kiehl's and Glashütte Original, among others.
