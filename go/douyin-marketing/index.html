Douyin is not TikTok with Chinese subtitles. It has its own algorithm logic, creator ecosystem, cultural codes and audience expectations. Brands that treat it as a repurposing channel for global content consistently underperform against those that build for it from scratch.

As a specialist Douyin agency, Qumin works with Western consumer brands to develop strategies, content and creator partnerships that are native to the platform. That means understanding what Douyin rewards, which creators carry genuine influence in your category, and how to connect short-form video to a coherent brand story in China.

We have operated across Douyin for brands in fashion, beauty, lifestyle, watches and consumer goods. Our work ranges from brand account launches to long-running creator programmes, always with platform expertise and brand integrity at the centre.

Common questions

What does a Douyin agency actually do for a Western brand?

A specialist Douyin agency handles everything from platform strategy and content production to KOL management and account operations. For Western brands, that also means bridging the gap between your global brand identity and what resonates with Chinese consumers on Douyin specifically.

How is Douyin different from TikTok, and why does that matter for our brand?

Douyin and TikTok share a parent company but operate as entirely separate platforms with different algorithms, user behaviours, creator ecosystems and cultural references. Content that performs on TikTok rarely translates to Douyin without significant reworking. Brands that treat them as interchangeable typically see weak results.

How do you choose the right KOLs and creators for a Douyin campaign?

Creator selection on Douyin depends on your category, your brand positioning and the specific objective of each campaign. Qumin evaluates creators on audience authenticity, content quality, category relevance and platform performance, not just follower counts. We brief, manage and maintain relationships with creators to ensure the output reflects your brand accurately.

We are entering China for the first time. Should Douyin be our starting point?

For most Western consumer brands, Douyin is one of the highest-priority platforms in China given its scale, its role in shaping purchase intent and its reach across age groups. Whether it is your first channel or part of a multi-platform launch depends on your category and audience.

Do you manage Douyin accounts on an ongoing basis or just run individual campaigns?

Both. Qumin works with brands on a retained basis, managing Douyin accounts month to month including content, community and performance reporting. We also run defined campaign projects such as product launches, seasonal activations or KOL programmes. Many clients start with a campaign and move to ongoing management once they see what consistent, expert-led activity delivers on the platform.
