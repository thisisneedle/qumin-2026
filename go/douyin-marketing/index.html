Why Douyin Marketing Demands a China-First Approach

Douyin is not TikTok with Chinese subtitles. It is a distinct platform with its own creator economy, content culture, trending formats and audience expectations. Western brands that treat it as a global channel adaptation almost always miss. The brands that win on Douyin build content and campaigns specifically for where Chinese consumers are in their relationship with a brand, from first discovery to deep loyalty.
