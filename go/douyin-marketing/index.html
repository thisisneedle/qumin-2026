Douyin is not TikTok with Chinese subtitles. It is a distinct cultural ecosystem where entertainment, community, and brand discovery are inseparable. Western brands that treat it as a content distribution channel miss the point entirely. Success on Douyin depends on understanding what Chinese consumers find genuinely compelling, not what performed well on Instagram or YouTube.

Effective Douyin marketing means building a brand voice that feels native without losing what makes your brand distinctive. That takes deep platform knowledge, strong creator relationships, and a content team that thinks in Douyin-first formats from the very first brief.
